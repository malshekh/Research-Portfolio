# TDP-43 Isoforms
## Overview
The human **TARDBP** gene undergoes alternative splicing, producing multiple transcript variants. According to the reviewed UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot entry, **two protein isoforms** are currently annotated.

Alternative splicing may influence protein localization, RNA-binding properties, and interactions with other proteins. Although the canonical full-length isoform is the predominant form studied in FTLD and ALS, alternative isoforms have also been implicated in disease mechanisms.

## Biological Significance

Alternative splicing increases protein diversity from a single gene. While most FTLD and ALS research focuses on the canonical 414-amino-acid protein, alternative isoforms are important for understanding TDP-43 biology, cellular localization, and disease-associated dysfunction.
